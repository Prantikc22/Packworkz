# Market pricing benchmark — August 2026

This note records the public prices used to calibrate Packworkz self-serve pricing. Public marketplace prices are market signals, not substitutes for signed supplier quotations. Final landed-cost records must include inward freight, packing, expected rejection and wastage before a SKU is released for sale.

## Public benchmarks observed

| Product | Public market signal | Packworkz decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Round paper stickers | Kraftix: 25 pieces at Rs 219 | Keep a 25-piece launch tier. Packworkz checkout is approximately Rs 239 including PACK7, listed delivery and GST for the standard 4 cm option. |
| Square / rectangular stickers | Kraftix: 25 pieces at Rs 199 | Keep 25-piece trials and lower rates through quantity breaks. Standard Packworkz checkout starts around Rs 225-246 including PACK7, listed delivery and GST. |
| Transparent stickers | Kraftix: 25 pieces at Rs 179 | Keep the entry tier close to the market, but retain a landed-cost floor because clear stock and white-ink coverage can materially change supplier cost. |
| Vinyl stickers | Kraftix: 25 pieces at Rs 336 | Packworkz can price below the observed public benchmark for standard sizes while routing unusual cuts and heavy ink coverage to review. |
| Custom paper boxes | Kraftix: 10 pieces at Rs 266 | Treat very small box runs as sampling or digital short runs. Production quantities retain fixed sizes and quantity breaks. |
| Printed paper bowls | Aaharpack: 10,000 at Rs 3.05; 20,000 at Rs 2.75; 30,000 at Rs 2.42; 50,000 at Rs 2.20, before GST, freight, lids and plate charges | Direct custom print begins at 10,000 per size/design. Plain stock starts at 300 and applied-label branding at 500. Lids and print setup remain explicit. |
| Plain paper bowls | EcoBowls: 500 ml at Rs 8.50, MOQ 300 | Keep the plain stock path from 300 pieces and size-based pricing. |
| Paper food containers | TradeIndia/Foodpak public listings vary roughly from Rs 4.50 to Rs 10.50 by capacity, often at 500-5,000 MOQ | Use size multipliers and landed-cost protection. Supplier-specific certification, non-standard capacity and direct print move to managed review. |

## Pricing controls

1. `PACK7` is automatically applied to the first 100 paid orders each calendar month; the server confirms availability.
2. Every payable self-serve configuration is checked against a 35% product gross-margin floor after the promotion.
3. Loaded landed cost adds 3% expected rejection/wastage, 3% inward freight and 2% handling/packing to the supplier cost record.
4. Custom dimensions, unusual print coverage, certifications, regulated formats and enterprise thresholds route to managed review.
5. Resin, paper and freight-sensitive families need monthly supplier review; other families need quarterly review.
6. Prices are shown in centimetres to customers. Millimetres may still be retained internally for production specifications and dielines.

## Customer account sequence

Checkout is guest-first. A customer pays without creating an account, receives an order reference, and can track with that reference plus the checkout email or mobile number. Account creation is offered after payment; the customer can then link the order and earlier quotes into the dashboard for production tracking and repeat ordering.
