# Pouch SKU coverage audit

**Audit date:** 28 September 2026  
**Reference set:** Nexibles “Types of Pouches” and “Nexi Classic” shop pages  
**Purpose:** Check whether Packworkz exposes the established pouch constructions buyers are likely to search for. This is a format coverage audit, not a recommendation to copy another supplier's size, laminate or price.

## Coverage after the update

| Established construction | Packworkz public family | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stand-up pouch | `FP-101` Stand-up Pouch | Covered |
| Spout pouch | `FP-104` Spout & Refill Pouch | Covered |
| Centre-seal pouch | `FP-102` Centre-seal Pillow Pouch | Exposed and renamed for search clarity |
| Quad-seal pouch | `FP-108` Quad-seal Pouch | Exposed as its own family |
| Centre-seal side-gusset pouch | `FP-107` Centre-seal Side-gusset Pouch | Exposed as its own family |

| Shape pouch | `FP-114` Custom Shaped Pouch | Covered |

## What changed

- The original catalog technically contained quad-seal and side-gusset records, but both were excluded from the storefront and represented only as options within the broad flat-bottom family. They are now discoverable product families.
- “Pillow pouch” is now labelled “Centre-seal Pillow Pouch”, matching the construction term used by procurement teams.
- The enterprise roll-fed offer is now labelled “Flow-wrap & Centre-seal Rollstock” so it remains distinct from a premade centre-seal pouch.
- A two-side seal family was added. It stays on the specialist-quote path because dimensions, open edge, laminate, filling method and seal validation change the commercial.
- The two-side seal page uses a new unbranded construction-specific catalog image generated for Packworkz; it does not reuse a competitor asset.

## Evidence used

The Nexibles reference range names eight core constructions: spout, stand-up, centre seal, quad seal, centre-seal side gusset, two-side seal, three-side seal and shape pouch. Its shop also shows fixed standard-size variants, including a 95 × 80 mm two-side seal pouch, a 125 × 175 mm three-side seal pouch, two centre-seal sizes and two centre-seal side-gusset sizes.

The competitor's displayed retail price was used only as a reasonableness check for the new two-side seal estimate. Packworkz keeps this product quote based; the competitor's commercial is not represented as a Packworkz price promise.
